Features

The dashboard is the product

Computed numbers first. Narrative second. Nothing rendered that wasn’t verified.

Net-worth dashboard

Invested vs current, absolute and percent. Allocation by asset type, sector and market cap. A sortable holdings table with honest nulls — an unknown cost basis renders —, never a fake −100%.

Daily history you can trust

One snapshot a day — net worth at Indian market close, all sources settled — captured ~23:45 IST, after mutual-fund NAVs publish. A missed snapshot can never be backfilled, so staleness is shown, not hidden: “History paused — last captured N days ago.”

An AI overview that shows its work

Every metric is computed in Python; the agents may not invent figures, and every claim carries its number — You are 47% financials. HHI 0.31. Largest holding 22% of net worth (illustrative). If the LLM is down, “AI overview unavailable” — and every number still renders.

How it works

Five steps. One consent.

From waitlist to a nightly-verified net worth.

01

Join the waitlist

AlphaDesk is a public URL, gated by a waitlist. Register interest and you’re approved in batches — no open sign-up at launch.

02

Sign in

A normal account sign-in. Your platform identity stays separate from your broker credential — linking comes later, and only with consent.

03

Read the consent screen ⏸ unskippable by design

Before any broker redirect, one screen names exactly what will be read, why, where it’s stored, and how to delete it. Not a checkbox buried in sign-up.

04

Link IND Money — read-only

A standard OAuth link that can only read. Tokens are encrypted at rest and never reach your browser; unlinking revokes access upstream, not just locally.

05

Dashboard, then nightly snapshots

Your net worth, allocation and holdings render immediately. Every night at ~23:45 IST a snapshot records net worth at market close, building the daily history and trend.

No cross-source auto-merge: holdings are grouped by instrument for display, and AlphaDesk asks before combining across sources — a silently wrong net worth is the one error a user cannot catch.
