From the markets desk — every figure checked before press

AlphaDesk is a portfolio analyzer for Indian retail investors. Sign in — a public URL, waitlist-gated — link your IND Money account read-only, and the ledger sets itself: invested against current, allocation by asset type, sector and market cap, every holding sortable on one page. Where the record is incomplete, the desk says so; where a number prints, it has been computed and checked first.

And the paper keeps its archive. Each night at about 23:45 IST, after mutual-fund NAVs publish, a snapshot goes to bed: your net worth at Indian market close, all sources settled — one row a day, compounding into a history and a trend you can actually read. Over it all, an AI overview narrates the verified numbers. It describes what is; it never tells you what to buy.

Public Notice

Notice No. AD/FRAME/2026-27/001 · Dated 16-08-2026

  1. This paper prints descriptive analytics only — an account of what is: "You are 47% financials. HHI 0.31. Your largest holding is 22% of net worth." (Figures illustrative.)
  2. No forward forecasts shall appear in these pages. The desk reports the record; it does not predict the tape.
  3. No buy or sell advice on real holdings, ever. The Lab's simulated calls stay in the Lab, on a paper watchlist, and are never printed beside your actual portfolio.
  4. No cross-source auto-merge. Holdings from different sources are never silently combined — the desk asks first. A silently wrong net worth is the one error a reader cannot catch.
  5. Every figure the AI cites is computed first and shown beside the claim. No agent may invent a number; where the record is missing, the desk prints "—".

The Lab

Inside pages · a research desk run entirely in simulation — plainly labelled, and kept off the front page.

A separate room, behind a marked door

Beyond the Markets page, AlphaDesk keeps a laboratory: the original multi-agent research desk, preserved as a simulation. A Scanner reads the day's NSE movers, a Research agent works the fundamentals, an Analyst files a report per candidate — thesis, confidence, action — and a Risk desk applies its guardrails before anything moves.

Then the presses stop at a human approval gate. Approved names go to a paper watchlist — and no further. Zero real orders have ever been placed here, and zero ever will be: there is no broker on the other end of the wire.

Simulation The Lab is a labelled simulation and is never fused with your real portfolio view. Its calls never appear beside your actual holdings, and they are not investment advice.
