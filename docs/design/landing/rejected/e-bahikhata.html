The Book

Heads of account

One book. Every paisa under its head.

A/C 01Net worth

Invested against current, in rupees and in percent — the balance every other page of this book carries forward.

A/C 02Allocation

Weighed by asset type, by sector, by market cap — which columns of the book your money actually sits in.

A/C 03Holdings

Every position in one sortable table. Where cost basis is unknown, the ledger shows "—" — never a fabricated −100%. An honest blank beats a confident wrong number.

A/C 04The daily entry

Captured about 23:45 IST, after mutual-fund NAVs publish: your net worth at Indian market close, all sources settled. One line a day, in ink.

A/C 05The running history

Daily lines become the trend — here, "carried forward" is literal. If capture pauses, the page says so: "History paused — last captured N days ago." A missed day can never be backfilled.

A/C 06The munim's commentary

An AI overview: four clerks — allocation critic, concentration risk, SIP health, performance attribution — hand notes to a synthesizer. Every figure is computed in Python first; no agent may invent one, and each claim shows its number. If the AI is unavailable, the dashboard still renders complete — a munim's ledger stands even when the commentary stops.
