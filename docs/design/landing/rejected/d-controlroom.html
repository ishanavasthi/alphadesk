The Lab · Simulation

The research desk, demoted to the Lab

The multi-agent research desk still runs — as a clearly labelled simulation in its own room, never fused with your real portfolio view. Its picks land on a paper watchlist and nowhere else.

  1. 01
    Scan

    The Scanner sweeps NSE movers and posts a shortlist of candidates to the desk.

  2. 02
    Research

    The Research agent pulls fundamentals and price context for each candidate over the read-only market-data line.

  3. 03
    Report

    The Analyst writes a full report per pick — thesis, risks, confidence score, stance.

  4. 04
    Risk check

    The Risk Manager applies hard guardrails — minimum confidence, sector caps, auto-reject on any analyst "avoid". Below the line means rejected, no overrides.

  5. 05
    You approve — or nothing happens

    The pipeline halts at the interlock and waits for you. Approved picks go to the paper watchlist — a simulation record, nothing more. Real orders placed: zero, by design.

Lab Interlock SIMULATION ONLY · 0 REAL ORDERS — EVER
Human approval — always on Guarded master switch, welded in the ON position. This switch has no off position: the Lab's approval gate is compiled into the pipeline, not a setting — and even approved picks only ever reach a paper watchlist.
